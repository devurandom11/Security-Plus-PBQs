Instructions

Open Scenario in the footer to read the briefing and the constraint recorded for each finding. Then complete the findings table.

How to answer

  1. Choose. Use the drop-down in the Most effective mitigation column of each row to select one mitigation technique for that finding.
  2. Keyboard. Tab to a drop-down and use the arrow keys or type the first letter to pick an option.
  3. Change your mind. Pick a different option in the same drop-down. Reset clears every row.

Every drop-down offers the same list. An option may be used more than once or not at all.

Scoring

Seven rows are scored and each correct selection earns one point. You have three attempts. The answer key appears after a perfect score or after the third attempt.

Briefing and per-finding constraints for the Northwind remediation exercise.

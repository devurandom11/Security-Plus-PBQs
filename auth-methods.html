Instructions

Open Scenario in the footer to read the briefing and the six access requests. Then complete the review queue.

How to answer

  1. Two dropdowns per row. Choose the access control model or identity mechanism the request calls for, then the implementation detail that carries it out.
  2. Keyboard. Tab to a dropdown and use the arrow keys, or type the first letters of an option.

An option may be used in more than one row, and not every option is used. Choose Select to clear a dropdown. Reset clears every selection.

Scoring

Twelve selections are scored (two per request) and each correct selection earns one point. You have three attempts. The answer key appears after a perfect score or after the third attempt.

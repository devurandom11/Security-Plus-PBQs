Scenario

Briefing and recovery requirements for the Northwind resilience and recovery planning exercise.

Northwind is rebuilding its recovery plan for four systems after a control-plane outage took every region of its cloud provider offline and a server failure on the plant floor stopped historian collection for four hours. The business impact analysis has set a recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO) for each system.

For each system choose a recovery site, a backup frequency, and a high availability approach.

Requirements

  1. Every selection must meet the system's RTO and RPO, measured from the loss of its primary site (for NW-ORDERS, its entire cloud provider). Where more than one option meets them, choose the least expensive. Each dropdown lists its options from most to least expensive by the prices quoted for Northwind's plan; this is the scenario's price ranking, not a general rule.
  2. Time to bring a recovery site into service: hot site minutes, warm site about 2 hours, cold site about 3 days. A cold site has no installed computing equipment and cannot host a running standby. Unless the system is already running at the recovery site, it must then be restored from backup there.
  3. An active-passive standby is maintained and running at the recovery site ahead of time, so it needs no site bring-up or restore; cutover to it takes 20 to 45 minutes. A failover cluster within one site moves the service to another node automatically within 5 minutes.

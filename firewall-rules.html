Scenario

Role briefing for the Northwind Campus perimeter firewall deployment.

Northwind Campus is replacing an aging perimeter firewall that had accumulated years of ad hoc exceptions. The replacement is a stateful appliance, and it is being brought up with an empty rule base so that the access policy can be rewritten from scratch rather than migrated.

The campus network is split into two zones behind the firewall. The screened subnet, 172.22.40.0/24, holds the four hosts that carry services other parties depend on: the customer portal, the inventory host, the resolver, and the outbound mail relay. Campus workstations sit on the internal LAN, 10.44.18.0/24, and reach the screened subnet only through the firewall.

Because the appliance is stateful, you only write the rule for the direction the session opens in. Return traffic for a permitted session is allowed automatically, so no rule pairs are needed and none should be added.

Facilities has signed off on the access policy in the Instructions pane, and it is the whole of the policy. Administration of the inventory host is restricted to one designated help desk workstation rather than the LAN as a whole, and the resolver serves campus devices only. Anything the policy does not name is out of scope and must fall through to the default deny at the bottom of the list.

The drop-downs offer values this firewall does not accept and values that are wider than the policy allows. Complete the list, then submit for scoring. You have three attempts.

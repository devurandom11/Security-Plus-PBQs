Scenario

Briefing and reference facts for the Northwind alert triage exercise.

You are the day-shift analyst in the Northwind security operations center on 12 August 2026. Five alerts from the intrusion detection sensors, the endpoint agent, and the web application firewall are waiting in the queue. The ruleset has not been tuned in two quarters, and your shift lead wants a classification and a response recorded against every alert, plus a tuning recommendation for the noisiest rule.

Requirements

  • Classify by this queue's convention. A true positive means the behavior the alert claims really occurred, even if it was authorized; that case is a benign true positive and is still recorded as True positive. A false positive means the suspicious behavior the alert claims is absent, even if the rule's pattern matched something. Other tools and standards may label authorized activity differently.
  • Choose the most specific response the evidence supports; do not ask for more collection when the evidence already settles the question.
  • A rule that is merely noisy is fixed in the tuning question, not in the response recorded against its alert.

Reference facts

  • NW-SCAN-01 (10.62.8.30) is the approved vulnerability scanner. Change CHG-4488 authorizes it to scan 10.62.40.0/24 every Tuesday 02:00 to 05:00.
  • NW-PORTAL-1 (172.20.10.17) is the public customer portal and sits behind the web application firewall.
  • NW-HD-05 (10.62.19.55) is the help desk support workstation. The SOC baseline records 40 to 70 remote desktop sessions from it per business day, all as svc_assist with MFA.
  • NW-FIN-07 (10.62.44.63) is a High-criticality finance workstation. Northwind Assist 5.4 is the only approved remote access agent, and no change record covers NW-FIN-07.
  • Rule NW-WEB-207 is meant to flag suspected SQL injection in HTTP headers, but its logic matches the literal substring select anywhere in any HTTP header. The field service app has sent the product token select-sync/1.4 in its User-Agent since version 3.0.
  • Rule NW-RDP-052 fires on any source that opens remote desktop sessions to many hosts, regardless of direction. Of its 1,975 events in 30 days, 1,821 came from NW-HD-05, all authenticated as svc_assist with MFA; the remaining 154 include every malicious true positive it has produced, and the two confirmed intrusions were both internal hosts fanning out to other internal hosts after an initial compromise.

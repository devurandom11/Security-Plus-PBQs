How to order the steps

  1. Buttons. Use Move up and Move down on a row. The position number updates as you go.
  2. Drag and drop. Drag a step's name or grip with a mouse or touch, then drop it on the row whose position it should take. Drag near the top or bottom of the screen to scroll. Dropping outside the list or pressing Escape cancels the move.
  3. Keyboard. Tab to a row's buttons and press Enter.

How to classify actions

Each row's Step column has a drop-down. Choose one step for each recorded action. A step may be used more than once, and not every step is used.

Reset restores the starting order and clears every drop-down.

Scoring

Thirteen items are scored: seven positions in the step order and six action classifications, one point each. Partial credit applies. You have three attempts. The answer key and explanations appear after a perfect score or after the third attempt.

    Scenario

    Briefing and requirements for the Northwind incident response exercise.

    An attacker signed in to the Northwind VPN with a stolen service account, enumerated two file servers, and began encrypting documents before the response team cut the session. The incident is now closed, and you are writing the after-action package for the security manager.

    Requirements

    1. Arrange the seven incident response steps in the order the process runs, first to last, following the SY0-701 model.
    2. For each recorded action, choose the single step it belongs to, judged by what the action accomplishes.
    3. A step may apply to more than one action, and some steps may not apply to any.

Practice PBQs

Interactive exercises that work the way the performance based questions on the SY0-701 exam do: a short scenario, a drawing or console to work in, and a scored task. The scenarios are set at Northwind, a company that does not exist.

  • One exercise is open now. The others are being reworked and will open as they are ready.
  • You get three attempts at each exercise, then the answer key and explanations.
  • Everything runs in your browser. Each exercise is a single HTML file and never sends anything to a server.

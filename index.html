Practice PBQs

Interactive exercises that work the way the performance based questions on the SY0-701 exam do: a short scenario, a drawing or console to work in, and a scored task. The scenarios are set at Northwind, a fictitious company.

  • Two exercises are open now. The others are being developed and will open as they are ready.
  • You get three attempts at each exercise, followed by an answer key and explanations.
  • To get the most out of these exercises, treat them as you would on the actual exam. Make your best attempt first, without reviewing any notes, then study the answer key and explanations thoroughly for any concepts that were difficult.

How to place items

  1. Drag and drop. Drag a device onto a numbered slot, or a server onto a network segment.
  2. Click to place. Click a device or server, then click the slot or segment.
  3. Keyboard. Tab to an item and press Enter to select it, then Tab to a slot or segment and press Enter.

A device may be used in more than one slot, and not every device is used. To remove an item, drag it from the drawing back to its Devices or Servers choice bank, or click the placed item and confirm Remove. With the keyboard, focus the placed slot or server and press Enter for confirmation, or Delete or Backspace to remove it. Reset clears everything.

Scoring

Nine placements are scored (seven slots and two servers) and each correct placement earns one point. You have three attempts. The answer key and completed drawing appear after a perfect score or after the third attempt.

Scenario

Briefing and design requirements for the Northwind Riverbend secure network design exercise.

Northwind is rebuilding the network at its Riverbend site. The site has about 1,150 office users, a customer-facing order portal, and a team of administrators who manage the servers remotely.

A recent assessment found that the order portal is vulnerable to SQL injection, unmanaged laptops can join the office network, administrators connect to servers directly from their own laptops over a shared VPN with no second factor, and outbound web activity cannot be tied to a user. The change board wants server traffic monitored for attacks but will not allow any new inline device on the server segment.

You have been given the site drawing with seven empty device slots. Place the correct security device in each slot and place the two portal servers in the correct network segments.

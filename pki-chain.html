Scenario

Briefing and requirements for the Northwind certificate validation exercise.

Northwind runs a two-tier internal PKI: an offline root CA signs two issuing CAs, and the issuing CAs sign the certificates for internal and partner-facing services. This morning four services fail certificate validation. Nothing on the network changed and no key was compromised, so each cause is in the certificate itself.

Build the chain of trust that validates the customer portal's certificate, then classify each failing service so the platform team can act.

Requirements

  1. Validate everything as of the system date, 2026-08-14 09:30 UTC.
  2. Position 1 is the certificate for portal.northwind.example, position 2 is the CA that signed it, and position 3 is the trust anchor already held by managed clients.
  3. Match a certificate to its issuer by comparing its issuer name and authority key identifier with the CA's subject and subject key identifier.
  4. Managed devices trust Northwind Root CA R2 only. Contractor laptops are unmanaged, trust only the public root programs (which contain no Northwind CA), and Northwind cannot install anything on them.
  5. Clients match the hostname against the subject alternative name only; the common name is ignored.
  6. Every current browser rejects a certificate whose signature uses SHA-1.
  7. The revocation status printed on each certificate was checked at 09:15 UTC; good means not revoked.
  8. For each finding choose the diagnosis the certificate supports and the single remediation that fixes that defect, not a control that merely improves the deployment.

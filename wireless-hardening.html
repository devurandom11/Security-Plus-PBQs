Scenario

Briefing and requirements for the Northwind wireless hardening exercise.

Northwind is rebuilding wireless controller NW-WLC-1 with three SSIDs. NW-CORP serves 410 managed laptops and phones plus the floor printers and display receivers that employees print and cast to. Every NW-CORP client, printers and display receivers included, is WPA3 certified, runs an 802.1X supplicant, and holds a certificate from the internal CA, which the existing RADIUS servers validate. NW-GUEST serves visitors, who are given a short passphrase printed at reception and rotated daily; every guest device seen in the site survey is WPA3 certified. NW-IOT-LEGACY serves 42 sensors and thermostats that report to an on-premises collector; all 42 support WPA2 with AES (CCMP), but none has an 802.1X supplicant or supports SAE, and 36 run end-of-support firmware that fails to associate when protected management frames are required (the other six support 802.11w). The site survey recorded repeated deauthentication floods from the parking structure.

Complete every dropdown cell in the controller configuration.

Requirements

  1. Every SSID must encrypt traffic over the air, and no deprecated protocol may be used.
  2. Each SSID must use the strongest mode that every client on that SSID can actually run.
  3. Corporate clients authenticate with their certificates, and no password-based credential may be used. Server certificate validation must be enforced by the managed Wi-Fi profile; users must not be prompted to trust a server certificate.
  4. The guest handshake must resist offline dictionary attacks against a captured handshake.
  5. Management frames must be protected for every client that supports 802.11w without dropping any client.
  6. Employees must print to floor printers and cast to displays on their own SSID; no guest or sensor may reach another client on its SSID.
  7. Corporate clients need internal services and the internet; guests reach the internet only; sensors reach only the on-premises collector.
